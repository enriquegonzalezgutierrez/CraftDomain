## 🌌 PART I: THE METAPHYSICS OF VOXEL MATTER

In the universe of Dolores, matter is not composed of atoms, but of **Chrono-Filaments** (threads of space-time code) woven into a rigid tridimensional matrix known as **The Voxel Grid**. 

```text
[ Voxel Coordinate ] ──► [ Chrono-Filament Tension ] ──► [ Density & Solidity ]
															   │
										 ┌─────────────────────┴─────────────────────┐
										 ▼                                           ▼
							  [ Crystalline State (Stone) ]               [ Volatile State (Lava) ]
```

Every block in the world is a physical crystallization of a specific state of time. The density, transparency, and physical behavior of a material are determined by the tension of these filaments:

*   **The Crystalline State (The Past):** Filaments are tightly locked in a geometric, parallel lattice. This creates immutable, highly durable materials (like Stone and Bricks) that resist the unweaving decay of the Null.
*   **The Organic State (The Present):** Filaments are flexible, continuously loops, and mutating. This creates biological, harvestable materials (like Wood, Grass, and Crops) that can change form, grow under sunlight, and conduct vital life forces.
*   **The Volatile State (The Nether):** Filaments are chaotic, vibrating at high frequencies, and constantly breaking and reforming. This creates liquid, highly unstable elements (like Water and Lava) that flow and fuse upon contact.

## 🪵 PART II: COMPREHENSIVE MATERIALS CATALOGUE

---

### 1. Chrono-Stone (The Element of Immature Memory - `ID 1`)
*   **Alchemical Composition:** 80% Basalt Crystalline Lattice, 20% Hardened Clay Shards.
*   **Metaphysical Meaning:** Representing the permanent weight of the Past. It is cold, inert, and carries the memory of the deep tectonic crusts before the creation of life.
*   **Alchemical Interaction:** If exposed to Geothermal Lava, it melts into a flowing state. If struck by the **Chrono-Scythe**, its immutable lattice vibrates, producing high-frequency stone debris.

---

### 2. Organic Humus (The Element of Change - `ID 2 / ID 3`)
*   **Alchemical Composition:** 50% Decayed Organic Cellulose, 30% Moist Sand Shards, 20% Living Spores.
*   **Metaphysical Meaning:** Representing the fertile ground of the Present. It is soft, loose, and acts as the primary medium for organic growth. Grass (`BLOCK_GRASS`) is a living skin; under the light of the Sun, its spores sprout and crawl onto adjacent dirt blocks, converting cold soil into lush, breathing plains.
*   **Alchemical Interaction:** Highly vulnerable to the purple temporal bleed of the Null. If corrupted, its living spores die, converting the block into dry, terracotta sand.

---

### 3. Chrono-Oak Wood (The Life Conductor - `ID 4 / ID 29`)
*   **Alchemical Composition:** 60% Fibrous Cellulose Conduits, 40% Vital Sap Shards.
*   **Metaphysical Meaning:** The vertical pillars of the Present. Wood is a natural conductor of vital space-time energy; it grows upwards towards the light of Logos, connecting the earth with the stratosphere.
*   **Alchemical Interaction:** Highly versatile. Planks (`BLOCK_OAK_PLANKS`) are lightweight and flexible, making them the ideal material for building scaffolds, gliders, and handles of weapons.

---

### 4. Geothermal Lava (The Volatile Reactor - `ID 15`)
*   **Alchemical Composition:** 90% Volatile Liquid Magma, 10% Molten Sulfur Shards.
*   **Metaphysical Meaning:** The lifeblood of the Nether Breach. It is highly unstable, radiant, and carries a chaotic time frequency that destroys organic matter.
*   **Alchemical Interaction (The Geothermal Fusion):** If Lava touches Water (`BLOCK_WATER`), their opposing space-time frequencies neutralize each other instantly. The chaotic vibration stops, freezing both liquids into a solid, inert block of **Chrono-Stone** with a loud, sizzling steam release.

---

### 5. Quantum Obsidian (The Silicon Conduit - `ID 12 / ID 13`)
*   **Alchemical Composition:** 70% Crystalline Volcanic Glass, 30% High-Frequency Cyber-Data.
*   **Metaphysical Meaning:** The building blocks of the Future era. Obsidian is a dark glass that has been artificially fused with digital energy conduits (Neon Cian and Magenta), creating an ultra-durable, emissive material that conducts calculations instead of heat.
*   **Alchemical Interaction:** Completely impervious to standard mining tools. It can only be fractured using diamond-tipped drills or high-frequency chronal energy.

---

### 6. Luminous Glowstone (The Radiant Vector - `ID 30`)
*   **Alchemical Composition:** 80% Crystalline Phosphorus, 20% Concentrated Solar Radiation.
*   **Metaphysical Meaning:** The pure light of the Future. Glowstone radiates a constant, warm golden halo that contains data patterns. It does not need fuel to burn; it shines eternally, accelerating the photosynthesis of nearby crops.

## 🛠️ PART III: THE TRANSMUTATION RECIPES OF THE LOOM

These formulas are used at the **Blueprint Workshop** to reshape the space-time code of materials, translating raw elements into valuable survival tools:

```text
			   [ Raw Past Element ] + [ Volatile Reactor ]
									│
									▼
					  [ ALCHEMICAL TRANSMUTATION ]
									│
									▼
						 [ Purified Present Product ]
```

### 1. Organic Composting (Leaves to Dirt)
*   **Alchemical Formula:** `3x Shrubbery Leaves` ➔ `1x Dirt Block`
*   **Metaphysical Process:** The player takes decaying, tattered leaves (Present) and accelerates their natural timeline of decomposition. By compressing the organic cellulose, they revert the volatile leaves into a dense, fertile block of organic soil.

### 2. Soil Pulverizer (Stone to Dirt)
*   **Alchemical Formula:** `1x Chrono-Stone` ➔ `3x Dirt Block`
*   **Metaphysical Process:** Using the crushing weight of a copper mallet, the player fractures the immutable, parallel lattice of the Stone (Past). The shattered basalt fragments are mixed with air and moisture, breaking the rigid historical memory into three blocks of loose, fertile soil (Present).

### 3. Geothermal Charcoal Fuel (Wood + Lava)
*   **Alchemical Formula:** `6x Wood Logs` + `1x Lava Bucket` ➔ `3x Geothermal Charcoal`
*   **Metaphysical Process:** Wood logs (organic Present) are submerged into a sealed crucible of volatile geothermal lava. The intense, chaotic heat of the lava purges all moisture and impurities from the wood, carbonizing it into high-efficiency charcoal that retains the volcanic heat eternally.

### 4. Igneous Cobbling (Dirt + Lava to Stone)
*   **Alchemical Formula:** `4x Dirt Blocks` + `1x Lava Bucket` ➔ `4x Chrono-Stone`
*   **Metaphysical Process:** Loose, active soil blocks are smelted directly in a stream of volatile geothermal lava. The chaotic energy of the lava binds the organic humus, freezing the flexible present-filaments into a rigid, parallel crystalline lattice, creating four blocks of durable, immutable Stone.
